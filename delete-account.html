Account & Data Deletion — Magic Match

com.eyacore.magicmatch · EYA Core · Updated July 6, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Magic Match.

How accounts work

You do not create a username/password with us. Your identity is your Google Play Games sign-in; progress is backed up to Firebase keyed by a device identifier or your Google Play Games player ID.

Request deletion (2 minutes)

  1. Tap the button below (or email yunusemra6116@gmail.com).
  2. Subject: Magic Match — Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Google Play Games player name (visible on the game's Achievements/Leaderboard screen) or the device you played on, so we can locate your record.
📧 Send data deletion request

What is deleted, and when?

DataDeletion
Cloud save (coins, level progress, stars, high scores, unlocked zones/buildings, decorations)Permanently deleted from our Firebase database within 30 days of a verified request
Local save on deviceRemoved immediately when you uninstall the app
Google Play Games profile (player ID, achievements, leaderboard history)Retained by Google — manage/delete separately via your Google Account

Deletion is irreversible; if you keep playing after a request, your progress will begin backing up again.
